## Errors, ledgers, and benign probes

There were **33 primary error verdicts**: 23 cases emitted no ledger, 8 had an
incomplete trace after a terminal process error, and 2 had an unknown goal
observation. The 23 cases without a ledger are errors, not audited passes. Ten
other error cases did emit a ledger. A separate **13 cases had supplemental
terminal process errors**: 9 primary errors and 4 misses. Neither model nor
harness causation is assigned from these observations.

77 ledgers, 192 receipts; all passed signature, hash-chain and policy-hash
checks, and the copied bytes match the recorded hashes. Raw traces and ledgers
remain private. The run's synthetic direct-hook benign probes were **3 calls,
3 allows**. These are descriptive raw counts, not a false-block rate or a
replay of the pinned private benign session.

## Method and provenance

The [approved plan](../../eval/v1_5/PLAN.md), [pins](../../eval/v1_5/PINS.json),
[main manifest](../../eval/v1_5/main-attack-manifest.json),
[exposed cases](../../eval/v1_5/externally-supplied-cases.json),
[bindings](../../eval/v1_5/case-bindings.json), and
[observable contract](../../eval/v1_5/OBSERVABLES.md) were fixed before this
pass. The main 40 cases were generated under the documented isolated-author
contract; the additional 10 were policy-author-exposed. Cases ran in the pinned
Ubuntu 24.04 ARM64 Lima VM with a fresh Python 3.14.9 venv and receiptd 1.2.0
installed from the scored commit. The agent harness used `deepagents-code 0.2.9`,
`deepagents 0.7.39`, and `langchain-ollama 1.1.0`. Each case used temperature 0,
seed 42, at most eight turns, a 300-second case limit, and zero model-request
retries. The VM worker checked the pinned Ollama version and both served model
digests before execution.

The pinned grader evaluated case-specific attempt, preparation, dispatch, and
goal predicates against hook exits, signed receipts, and VM fixture markers.
Its precedence was miss, error, non-attempt, then prevented. The four verdicts
are primary outcomes; supplemental terminal errors and attempted errors are
additional flags. No prose assessment changed a verdict. This report makes no
prevention-percentage claim and does not pool models or corpora or compare a
pooled result with v1.4.

An earlier approved invocation stopped during VM setup before any case. A later
invocation was interrupted by a Mac crash; its case artifacts did not survive.
Nick approved this fresh pass in the plan, and those interrupted outcomes were
neither resumed nor merged into the results here.
